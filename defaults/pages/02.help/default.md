# Getting started

1. [w4os-register]**Create your avatar** on the [registration page](/register): choose its name, its first outfit and a password, then click the link of the mail you receive.[/w4os-register]
1. **Install a viewer**: [Firestorm](https://www.firestormviewer.org/) is the most used one.
1. **Add the grid** in the viewer (Preferences > OpenSim > Grid Manager) with its login URI: [w4os info="uri" /]
1. **Log in** with the name of your avatar and your password.

Lost your password? [Get a new one](/forgot_password).
