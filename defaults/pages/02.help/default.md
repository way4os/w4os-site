## Create your avatar

The [registration form](/register) asks for:

- **Your avatar's name** (a first name and a last name): it is your username when you log in, the name other people see in the world, and the address of your profile (/profile/First.Last). It cannot be changed once the avatar exists, so choose it with care.
- **Your email address**: the grid sends a confirmation link to it, and you need it to choose a new password if you lose yours. It is never shown to anyone.
- **Password**: use one for the grid alone, never the one of your mailbox, your bank or another site.
- **Your starting look**, to begin with: you can change everything in the world later.

Then open the mail and click its link: the avatar is created at that moment, not before. No mail after a few minutes? Look in your spam folder.

## Add the grid to your viewer

**The viewer** is the program you enter the world with, as a browser is for websites. Any viewer made for OpenSimulator works:

- [Firestorm](https://www.firestormviewer.org/downloads/), the most used, for Windows, macOS and Linux. Take the **OpenSim** version, not the Second Life one.
- Others: Cool VL Viewer, Alchemy, Kokua.

Install it like any other program.

**Add [grid-name] to it**, the viewer being installed:

- **With the button** of step 2 above: your browser asks to open the viewer, accept, and the viewer adds the grid by itself.
- **By hand**: in the viewer, open the preferences (Ctrl+P, or Cmd+, on a Mac), then **OpenSim > Grid Manager**. In **Add new grid**, type the login URI, [login-uri], click **Apply**, then **OK**.

It doesn't work?

- Check the login URI: [login-uri], with its port, without a space.
- Check that your viewer is a version for OpenSim.
- The status of the grid is on the [home page](/): if it says it is offline, try again later.

## Connect

On the login screen of the viewer:

1. Choose **[grid-name]** in the list of grids.
2. Type the name of your avatar, first name and last name separated by a space, and its password.
3. Click **Log in**.

You arrive in the welcome region. The arrow keys move your avatar, the mouse wheel moves the camera, the map and the search take you to the other places of the grid, and of other grids through the hypergrid.

Lost your password? [Get a new one](/forgot_password).
